Executive Profile

Driving strategic transformation at scale

Ambuj Dhakad is a Senior Consultant at EY-Parthenon, where he advises enterprises on high-stakes operational and strategic agendas. Armed with an MBA from IIM Kozhikode (Top 15% of batch) and an Integrated M.Sc. from NISER, he combines elite strategy training with deep analytical rigor — and an entrepreneur's bias for execution.

His work sits at the intersection of operational transformation, direct cost reduction, and go-to-market strategy. He has delivered ₹25 Cr+ in annualized operational savings across enterprise engagements — including asset optimization and quality management for India's largest oil & gas producer — applying Lean Six Sigma methodologies and data-driven B2B expansion frameworks that convert analysis into P&L impact.

Prior to consulting, Ambuj co-founded GAMERS TAG, where he led finance and built Generative AI architectures using n8n automation. From steering factory turnarounds on the shop floor, to shaping fintech policy research at the Reserve Bank of India, to winning the Yale Graduate Case Competition internationally — his mandate remains constant: turn complex problems into measurable, durable growth.
